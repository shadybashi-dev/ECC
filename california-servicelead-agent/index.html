Turn service requests into booked estimates.

A simple inbound lead-intake agent for HVAC and plumbing companies. It asks the right questions, flags emergencies, checks the service area, and hands qualified requests to a human.

This is a demo. The agent does not send an SMS, call anyone, or dispatch a technician.

For a real deployment, the client must approve its privacy notice, consent language, service area, emergency policy, and CRM access.
What the agent does

Safe triage, not autonomous dispatch

Emergency or unsafe requests are never diagnosed by the demo. They are escalated to a human and shown in red.

1. Capture

Collect the minimum fields needed to respond.

2. Classify

Routine, soon, outside area, or human escalation.

3. Route

Recommend a follow-up action and store an audit event.

4. Measure

Show the owner how many requests were qualified and booked.

Production checklist

Before connecting a real client

  • Client-approved privacy and consent language.
  • Written data-processing/service-provider terms.
  • Human owner for emergencies and escalations.
  • Separate account and data for each client.
  • Usage cap, logs, deletion and rollback.
  • Test cases before any external message.
