PROMPT — ANTONIA'S PIZZA / "THE FUTURE OF DELIVERY IS HERE"
Master cinematic prompt (works for Wan 2.2, LTX-Video, HunyuanVideo 1.5)
========================================================================

MASTER / STYLE (prepend to every shot)
--------------------------------------
Cinematic Super Bowl commercial x sci-fi trailer, anamorphic 35mm, shallow depth of
field, volumetric light, film grain, warm golden-and-tomato-red grade. A giant premium
pizza box — warm cream kraft card, deep tomato-red band, gold-foil serif wordmark
"Antonia's Pizza" — lid cracked open, molten cheese stretching, thick golden steam
curling out. Crimson and amber spotlights cut through ground fog on a dark stage,
silhouetted audience at the edges, embers drifting. Futuristic reveal that still smells
like a real neighborhood pizzeria.

Single-clip version (if you have ONE 5-second slot)
---------------------------------------------------
Low-angle hero shot, slow motion: a giant cream-and-tomato-red Antonia's Pizza box with
gold-foil serif lettering hovers above a glossy black stage floor, lid open, molten
cheese stretching, golden steam curling. It rises, tilts and glides forward as crimson
and amber spotlights sweep through ground fog, embers trailing, stunned silhouetted
audience at the edges, anamorphic lens flare. Camera cranes up into a slow orbiting
drone move as the box banks. It settles onto a rustic wooden table and the lid opens to
a steaming 28-inch pepperoni pizza — bubbling mozzarella, charred seasoned crust, fresh
basil, an epic cheese pull. Warm pizzeria light overpowers the sci-fi darkness.
Photoreal, 24fps, cinematic grade, film grain.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (LTX-Video flag / Wan negative field)
-----------------------------------------------------
blurry, low quality, distorted text, misspelled lettering, warped logo, extra boxes,
melting cardboard, text artifacts, watermark, jitter, inconsistent motion, static shot,
cartoon, plastic look, oversaturated, duplicated audience, floating hands

NOTES
-----
- 5 seconds is the realistic ceiling per clip on open models. Generate 4 clips and cut.
- Put the exact string "Antonia's Pizza" in quotes in the prompt. It helps, it does not
  guarantee correct spelling — the real logo goes on in post-production.
- For image-to-video, use keyframe_hero.png as frame 0 and drop the "a giant ... box"
  description down to motion + camera only. Identity comes from the image, not the text.
- Seed 42 is a good starting point; change seed before changing the prompt.
