After-midnight tail — how our hours work

Our hours are stored as [open, close] in minutes-from-midnight per weekday. A close value above 1440 means the shift spills into next day (1560 = 2 AM). At 00:30 the date has rolled over, so the still-running shift belongs to yesterday's row — our JS checks that tail explicitly. Without it the site would read Closed during exactly the late-night hours the shop is known for.
