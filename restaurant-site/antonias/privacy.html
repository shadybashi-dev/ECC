The short version

privacy, in plain english

This website is a menu card, not a surveillance device. Here is exactly what happens to your data here — all of it.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no account system. We cannot sell what we never take.

The one thing we save

If you press the site's motion Pause button, that choice is kept in your own browser (local storage) so the site stays still next time. It never leaves your device. Clearing browser data clears it.

Where orders go

This site takes no orders and no payment. Order buttons hand you to Toast (antoniaspizza.toast.site), and the app link to OrderSave — those services handle your order under their own privacy policies.

Questions?

Call the kitchen you care about and a human will answer: San Luis Obispo (805) 439-2383 · Paso Robles (805) 238-1851. If this page ever stops matching what the site does, this page gets fixed — that is the whole policy.
